# Homepage

Scope: the single-page Bombay Palace site (`dist/index.html`). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: diners around Columbus, Indian and American, deciding on dinner; the owner reviewing it.
Action: explore the menu and get directions.
Proof: the selected menu, the address, and honest pending hours, phone, email, and services. Photographs stay illustrative and labelled.
Constraints: warm white ground, charcoal text, one deep-green accent, existing photographs kept whole, no invented story or services, noindex.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Bombay Palace is set like a Sunday dining feature. The square food photograph and a measured headline share one opening, then a scannable menu and a clear way to the door. It refuses the painted signboard, the beige field, the forest slab, brush rules, and any panel sitting on the food.

OWN-WORLD: warm white paper (#FBF9F6), charcoal ink (#1C1916), one deep green (#1B4332) for the action and the pressed state. Sorts Mill Goudy for the wordmark and headlines only; Source Sans 3 for navigation, the menu, and buttons. 2px corners. Hairline rules. Photographs keep their own proportion, with the name beside or beneath, never on the plate.

STORY: The visitor sees the food and the place, reads a short confirmed introduction, browses the real categories, scans the selection, meets the clay oven, looks through the gallery, and leaves with directions. Unconfirmed services stay absent.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A solid header holds the arch, the Goudy wordmark, four links, and Explore the menu. The headline spans the opening beside the square spread, which floats at its natural proportion with no crop and no card on it. Under the headline: Indian cuisine in Columbus, Georgia, the intro, Explore the menu, Get directions, and the address.

FORM: The Sunday dining page, position 3, seed:5982e7374688cebe. Raises: photograph and headline sized as a pair (from the bazaar spread); every picture named in the same breath and left uncropped (from the loteria); the wordmark stays put for the whole visit (from the room); unconfirmed prices and illustrative photos carry an honest ticket (from the moon bazaar); every dish uses one scannable line (from the archive). Signature interaction: a category photograph or filter presses that category, and a search that misses inside the filter says where the dish actually is. Motion: the gallery opens in a dialog; the page is fully visible with motion off.
